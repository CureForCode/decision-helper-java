      How it works

      Options are the alternatives you want to compare.

      Criteria are the aspects that matter (salary, rent, growth, etc.).

      Weight (1–5) = importance of a criterion.
      1 = low, 5 = critical.

      Score (1–10) = how good an option is for a criterion.

      Calculation:
      score × weight → sum all → winner
